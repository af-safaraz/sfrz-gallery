About

I'm an Indonesian photographer and videographer based in London. Found my passion for photography since 2007. I dedicate my life to the world of imagery with creativity, enthusiasm and an extremely keen eye. Have worked along with many well-known photographers in Indonesia. On my spare time, I like to play bass, casually vlogging and practically wandering around the street taking pictures.

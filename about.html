about

Hi, I’m Andi Fikri Safaraz — a software engineer by profession and a photographer by passion from a little district called Paser in East Kalimantan, Indonesia. My journey in photography truly began during my years studying Informatics Engineering at Brawijaya University in Malang. Between classes and projects, I found myself reaching for my camera in my free time—and it became a way for me to see the world differently, appreciating the beauty in both the grand and the mundane.

I’m particularly drawn to capturing candid moments—strangers going about their daily lives. There’s something so fascinating about capturing those unspoken stories—wondering what’s on their mind, what challenges they’re facing, or what adventures life has taken them on. It’s a constant reminder of the shared yet unique experiences that make life so fascinating.

This website is a blend of my two worlds. I built this site as a way to showcase my photography and my coding journey. It’s not the most advanced website, and it’s definitely far from perfect—I have some exciting vision for its future, and I’m still learning along the way. I’ll be updating the site soon as I continue to improve and grow my skills.

Thanks for stopping by! I hope you enjoy exploring my photos and the stories they tell. Feel free to reach out through my social media below.
